    Computer Science and Quantitative Methods: A Study Guide

    Worked questions, concept primers and algorithm notes for computer science, mathematics, machine learning and quantitative finance.

    Overview and how to use this guide

    This guide is organized by subject, not by day. Part 1 is core computer science, Part 2 mathematics and probability, Part 3 machine learning and decision-making, Part 4 quantitative finance, Part 5 software engineering and parallel computing. Algorithms are collected in Appendices A1 (core), A2 (systems and databases) and A3 (probability, learning and finance); each question links to the algorithm it uses, and each algorithm lists its steps.

    Every question has a worked answer. Cover the answer, try the question on paper, then compare. Each section opens with a concept primer that explains the ideas before the questions begin.

    Rule of thumb for each question: say the definition in a sentence, give a small example, state the answer or complexity, and say what could go wrong.

    20-day study plan

    Each day pairs about 2.5 hours of computer science with 2 hours of mathematics, learning or finance, plus 30 minutes of written practice. Do the questions on paper before reading the answers. Days 7, 14 and 19 are self-tests and review; Day 20 is light.
